stonetrack finds stones by handle colour and shape and tracks them on the ice plane, with no SAM and no masks. It calibrates the cameras from the paint itself (the landmarks above are not used, only the panel crops), always runs at 20 frames per second, and takes a few extra minutes the first time it sees a recording. A whole end is quick, so the interval can be Whole end.
