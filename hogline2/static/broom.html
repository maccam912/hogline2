Report

Line is the stone's heading just after release, carried to the broom's depth. Narrow means it left toward the side it then curled; wide is the other side. Clockwise is an in-turn for a right-hander. Weight is the hog-to-hog time, grouped by the called shot when one is labelled. Players come from the team lineups by delivery order.
